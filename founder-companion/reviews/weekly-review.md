# Weekly Founder Companion Review

Timebox: 45 minutes.

## Reality

- What gave energy?
- What consumed energy?
- Which family anchors were actually protected?
- What did sleep and movement look like?
- Which promise or relationship needs repair?

## Decisions

- Which important decision was made?
- Which prediction changed?
- Which decision remains open because a specific input is missing?
- Did the Companion surface a real conflict or merely produce agreeable prose?

## Compounding

1. Did one important uncertainty shrink?
2. Did one important relationship deepen?
3. Did a reusable system replace founder effort?
4. Was any success purchased with hidden health, family, or trust debt?
