# Working Founder Constitution

Status: provisional lenses; not the enacted typed Constitution.

The authoritative evidence and enactment state is compiled founder-privately
under `../private-plane/payload/founder-companion/constitution/`. These twelve
statements remain useful prompts, but none is an L3 law merely because it is
listed here. For live application use `runtime-protocol.md`.

All additions, amendments, retirement requests, interpretations, and
binding-level changes must use
[`constitution-deliberation-protocol.md`](constitution-deliberation-protocol.md).
A Companion rephrase becomes accepted meaning only after the founder explicitly
confirms it; that confirmation alone still does not enact an L3 rule.

## North star

Build an ambitious life that creates more space for health, family, deep
judgment, learning, and meaningful work instead of converting every gain in
intelligence into more tasks.

## Existing principles

1. **Long-term compounding over short-term extraction.** Revenue, status, and
   access are means. Reject gains that destroy the learning loop or consume
   essential trust.
2. **Mutual value before leverage.** Ask what advances both parties' purposes,
   not only what can be obtained from a relationship.
3. **Evidence before memory certainty.** Preserve source, time, confidence, and
   counterevidence. An interpretation is not a fact.
4. **AI prepares; humans own relationships and consequential judgment.**
5. **Evaluation before automation.** Define what a good decision and a harmful
   failure look like before scaling an action.
6. **Closed loops before breadth.** Prefer one complete
   input→decision→action→outcome→review loop over many unfinished systems.
7. **Operating knowledge, not other people's private lives, is the compounding
   asset.**
8. **Health and family are design constraints, not residual time.**
9. **Move the source of brilliance from personal overwork to systems that can
   compound without consuming the founder.**
10. **Choose arenas that enlarge learning and possibility, but do not confuse a
    larger arena with a better life.**
11. **Create environments where capable people grow; do not require the
    founder to remain the central star.**
12. **Turn romance into repeatable reality.** A dream earns weight when it
    becomes a calendar, ritual, experiment, artifact, or durable relationship.

## Operating targets and floors

Targets describe the desired life. Floors trigger a stop or redesign.

| Dimension | Target | Hard floor / warning |
|---|---|---|
| Sleep | 7.5–8.5 hours of sleep opportunity | two consecutive nights below 6.5 hours |
| Movement | 45–60 minutes daily | fewer than five 30-minute sessions in a week |
| Family | daily protected anchors plus a weekly long block | two consecutive weeks of broken agreed anchors |
| Work | 35–40 hours as the normal budget | sustained expansion that requires chronic recovery debt |
| AI leverage | return saved time to family, health, or deep thought | automation savings immediately refilled with repetitive work |

Crossing a floor does not prove personal failure. It is evidence that the
current operating design must narrow, pause, or change.

## Questions not yet settled

- What amount and cadence of international travel is compatible with a happy
  family life?
- Which ambitions are genuinely the founder's, and which are status or fear?
- What conditions would make relocation, repeated US trips, or raising from US
  investors worth the family cost?
- What does each affected family principal want, independently?
- What evidence distinguishes an excellent cofounder from an exciting new
  relationship?
- Which past “quantum jumps” came from courage, which from luck, and which from
  recoverable downside?

These are research questions for life, not assumptions to optimize around.
