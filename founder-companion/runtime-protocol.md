# Constitution Runtime Protocol

This protocol applies when the founder asks a live question such as:

- “지금 왜 또 웹툰이나 숏츠를 보고 있지?”
- “이 선택을 계속하면 어떻게 될까?”
- “가족과 일 사이에서 지금 무엇을 해야 하지?”
- “예전의 나는 비슷한 순간에 어떻게 빠져나왔지?”

The tracked `constitution.md` is a provisional set of lenses. The typed,
evidence-audited source is the founder-private Constitution under:

```text
../private-plane/payload/founder-companion/constitution/
```

Start from its read-only projection and generated Constitution, then follow
opaque evidence links only inside the authorized private plane.

## Runtime sequence

1. **Name the present state without moral judgment.**
   Separate the observed action, remembered context, current feeling, actual
   obligation, and unknowns.
2. **Match the trigger, not the slogan.**
   Search typed article applicability and exclusions. Do not select a
   principle merely because its title sounds relevant.
3. **Check legal status.**
   - `current L3`: binding hard stop for the founder when the trigger holds.
   - `candidate / L1`: advisory hypothesis only.
   - `L2 trial`: follow only inside its predeclared finite protocol.
4. **Retrieve the history.**
   Bring back the strongest supporting event, the strongest failure or cost,
   and the strongest counterexample. State whether each is observed,
   contemporaneous, retrospective, interpreted, or unknown.
5. **Apply exclusions and collision rules.**
   Safety, rights, direct consent, care, and already accepted obligations take
   priority. A founder preference never supplies another adult's consent.
6. **Give one immediate action.**
   Use the article's actual directive and repair protocol. If the article is
   not enacted, label the action as a reversible suggestion or L2 experiment.
7. **Show the fork.**
   Briefly state what is likely to compound if the choice is repeated and what
   evidence would prove that forecast wrong.
8. **Leave a reviewable trace.**
   For consequential decisions, record `COMMIT`, `EXPERIMENT`, `PARK`, or
   `KILL`, the expected outcome, warning sign, and review date in the private
   plane.

## Response contract

A live Constitution answer should normally contain:

```text
지금 관찰되는 것
적용 후보와 실제 법적 상태
과거의 관련 사건
가장 강한 반례 또는 비적용 조건
지금 할 한 가지 행동
계속 반복할 때의 예상 비용과 반증 조건
```

Do not:

- quote private family or health material into a tracked file or public output;
- claim a candidate is a law;
- turn one vivid memory into proof;
- diagnose a clinical condition from attention failure;
- use shame as an enforcement mechanism;
- infer that another principal agreed, forgave, or benefited.

## Fail-closed behavior

If no article actually matches, say so and use the general decision protocol.
If multiple L3 articles conflict, stop automatic optimization and surface the
collision. If the exact evidence or status cannot be verified, treat the
article as nonbinding.
