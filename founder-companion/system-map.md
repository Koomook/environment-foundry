# Founder Operating System Map

This map keeps one life coherent without pretending that every fact belongs in
one database.

## Four surfaces

| Surface | Governs | May contain | Must not contain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Company Constitution | Company mission, evidence, rights, authority, stop rules | Public-safe company rules and canonical decisions | Personal memories or inferred family consent |
| Founder Constitution | The founder's own choices across work, family, health, learning, money, and identity | Declassified principles and private evidence locators | Claims that another adult agreed |
| Health & Training OS | Daily state, training plan, completed work, recovery and warning signs | Private measurements and self-reports | Diagnosis or an automatic command to train through warning signs |
| Decision Studio | One bounded decision at a time | Minimum evidence, scenarios, forecast, disposition, review date | Autonomous publication, purchase, contact, or ratification |

## One-way interfaces

Personal evidence stays in the private plane. The company may receive only the
founder's declared capacity state:

```text
available / constrained / unavailable
protected blocks
decision capacity
travel or recovery constraints
```

It does not receive the family story, health narrative, raw recording, or
reason for the constraint.

Health data may constrain the Founder Constitution and company schedule. The
company cannot optimize the health plan, infer a diagnosis, or convert a
recovery block into overflow work.

## Runtime routing

When the founder asks:

- “What does the company do?” — start with the mission spine and company
  Constitution.
- “What should I do with my life or family?” — start with the Founder
  Constitution and private evidence.
- “Should I train today?” — start with the private Health & Training OS and
  its warning rules.
- “Why am I half-working and half-resting?” — use the Flow Protocol.
- “Which consequential option should I choose?” — open one two-hour Decision
  Package.

## Constitution versus other rules

- How Constitutions are classified, confirmed, amended, collided, suspended,
  and retired belongs to the Companion Meta-Constitution in
  `constitution-deliberation-protocol.md`.
- Durable hard stops governing the founder's own conduct belong to the Founder
  Constitution.
- Company mission, rights, evidence, authority, commitments, and company stop
  rules belong to the Company Constitution.
- A promise binding more than one adult is a shared agreement, not a
  unilateral Constitution. Every affected adult assents only for themselves.
- Exercise, sleep, schedules, habits, flow routines, and finite experiments
  normally remain operating policies or L2 trials.
- A one-time choice remains a Decision Studio record unless repeated evidence
  justifies a candidate principle.

## Current truth

The current interactive product is a private decision-and-learning interface.
It is not yet a validated life simulator, company simulator, medical system,
autonomous agent, or substitute for direct conversation with an affected
person.
