# Consequential Decision Protocol

## The two-hour package

```text
00–30  Prepare
30–60  Realtime Founder Companion dialogue
60–90  Human decision record
90–120 Protected buffer and recovery
```

The buffer is not overflow work. Walk, drink water, eat, or remain still.

## Prepare

- Write one decision question.
- Identify the decision owner and every affected principal.
- Load only the minimum authorized evidence.
- Name the current default and why it may be wrong.
- Define the decision date and whether delay is itself a choice.

## Dialogue

The Companion must ask:

1. What does the founder want?
2. What fear or status incentive may be disguised as principle?
3. What does each affected principal want, based on direct evidence?
4. Which option preserves reversibility and learning?
5. What would this choice look like after one, three, and ten years?
6. Which past event is genuinely analogous, and where does the analogy break?
7. What would a credible critic say?

## Record

Choose one disposition:

- `COMMIT` — evidence and authority are sufficient.
- `EXPERIMENT` — run a reversible test with a review date.
- `PARK` — wait for a named missing input.
- `KILL` — reject and record why it should not reappear unchanged.

The decision is incomplete without an expected outcome, warning signs, affected
principal checkpoint, and review date.

## Outcome review

At the review date, separate:

- what happened;
- what was caused or plausibly influenced by the decision;
- what remains unknown;
- whether the principle predicted well;
- whether hidden health, family, trust, or opportunity debt appeared.
