# Source ledger policy

The Companion may use the founder's authorized memories, calendars, decision
records, books, mentors, and outcome reviews. It must preserve the difference
between:

- direct contemporary evidence;
- later recollection;
- another person's stated preference;
- the founder's inference about another person;
- an external author's principle;
- a present forecast.

Tracked files may contain public citations and declassified abstractions.
Private evidence remains in the ignored payload plane and is referenced only
by opaque local locator.

A principle becomes durable only after it:

1. explains more than one independent event;
2. includes a meaningful counterexample;
3. changes a real decision;
4. survives an outcome review; and
5. does not externalize hidden cost to another principal.
