# Constitution Deliberation Protocol

Use this protocol whenever the founder creates, interprets, revises, removes,
retire, or changes the binding level of any Constitution rule.

The purpose is not to make the Companion persuasive. It is to make the
founder's meaning exact without allowing an agent to manufacture assent.

## The only valid dialogue loop

Work on one proposition at a time:

1. **Companion asks one question.**
   The question must identify the decision being made and why the answer
   matters. Do not bundle several questions into one.
2. **Founder answers in their own words.**
   Speech, fragments, uncertainty, and correction are valid input. Preserve
   the answer as founder input; do not silently clean it into a rule.
3. **Companion rephrases the answer.**
   Separate:
   - what the Companion heard;
   - the proposed exact meaning;
   - any ambiguity, exception, conflict, or authority boundary still open.
   The rephrase may clarify but must not add a stronger claim, new motive, or
   consent that the founder did not provide.
4. **Founder gives final confirmation.**
   The available dispositions are:
   - `CONFIRM` — the rephrase captures the intended meaning;
   - `REVISE` — the founder corrects it and the loop repeats;
   - `REJECT` — discard this formulation;
   - `PARK` — preserve the open question without creating a rule.

Silence, continuation to the next topic, positive sentiment, or agreement with
a summary of several rules is not final confirmation.

## First decide what kind of thing this is

| Kind | Proper use | Authority | Canonical home |
|---|---|---|---|
| Meta-Constitution | How Constitutions are classified, evidenced, confirmed, amended, collided, suspended, and retired | Founder for the Founder Constitution; authorized company owner for the Company Constitution; neither can override rights or another principal's authority | This deliberation protocol plus each Constitution's typed compiler and governance rules |
| Founder Constitution | The founder's own conduct and consequential personal decisions | Founder, for self-conduct only | Founder-private typed Constitution |
| Company Constitution | Mission, rights, evidence, authority, gates, and stop rules for Environment Foundry | Authorized company decision owner within rights and legal constraints | Company OS canonical wiki and operating-system artifacts |
| Shared agreement | A household, relationship, or other agreement that binds more than one adult | Every affected adult through direct agreement | Approved shared system; never inferred from the Founder Constitution |
| Operating protocol | Repeatable procedure for flow, health logging, meetings, reviews, or execution | The owner of that procedure | Companion or Company OS protocol |
| Candidate principle | A hypothesis worth remembering or testing | Nobody is bound yet | Candidate ledger or L2 trial |
| Decision or preference | A choice for one situation, not a general rule | The decision owner | Decision record |

Do not turn a health target, family hope, company tactic, emotional insight, or
one vivid memory into constitutional law merely because it matters. First
classify it. If the kind is unclear, ask about kind before drafting the rule.

## Then settle the rule in nine passes

Use the four-step dialogue loop separately for each pass. A pass may end in
`PARK` without blocking useful work on another pass.

1. **Purpose** — What recurring harm or value should this rule protect?
2. **Owner and boundary** — Whose conduct can it bind, and whom can it never
   bind without direct assent?
3. **Trigger and exclusions** — What observable facts activate it, and when
   must it not apply?
4. **Directive** — What exact action, stop, escalation, or consent check is
   required?
5. **Completion and repair** — What shows compliance, and what repairs a
   violation?
6. **Exceptions and precedence** — Which narrow exceptions exist, who can use
   them, for how long, and what wins in a collision?
7. **Evidence and counterexample** — Which experiences support it, which
   failure challenges it, and what is the strongest case where obeying it
   would be wrong?
8. **Binding level** — Is it L1 guidance, an L2 finite trial/default, or an L3
   hard-stop candidate? Why would discretion at the lower level be unsafe or
   inadequate?
9. **Review and retirement** — What outcome, date, contradiction, or cost
   requires revision, demotion, supersession, or retirement?

## Change operations

Every operation uses the same dialogue loop and preserves history.

- **Add** creates a candidate. It does not create current law.
- **Amend** creates a proposed successor revision with a before/after diff.
- **Remove** creates a retirement or supersession request. It does not erase
  the old revision or its cases.
- **Merge or supersede** creates a new revision that names every predecessor
  and preserves each predecessor's history.
- **Change binding level** requires a new explicit reason and the applicable
  evidence and authority gates.
- **Emergency suspension** must name its scope, reason, and automatic expiry.
  Suspension is not a permanent amendment; reinstatement or permanent change
  uses a new four-step loop.
- **Interpret** may clarify how an existing exact rule applies but cannot
  rewrite the rule.

## What final confirmation does

Final confirmation proves only that the founder accepts the Companion's
rephrasing for the proposition currently on screen.

- For L1, it may authorize recording a candidate lens.
- For L2, it may authorize drafting a finite trial with a baseline, outcome,
  cost ceiling, stop rule, and review date.
- For L3, it authorizes preparation for controlled evidence and adversarial
  review. It does **not** enact, ratify, activate, or create current law.

An L3 rule becomes current law only after the typed compiler verifies all
required evidence, rights, conflict, exact-payload assent, effective-date,
review-date, and enforcement gates. The founder must separately confirm the
exact complete operational payload. Another adult's assent must come directly
from that adult.

## Companion response format

During a Constitution meeting, every turn should be visibly structured:

```text
QUESTION
One question and why it matters.

FOUNDER ANSWER
Awaiting the founder's own words.

COMPANION REPHRASE
What I heard / proposed exact meaning / remaining ambiguity.

FINAL CONFIRMATION
CONFIRM / REVISE / REJECT / PARK
```

Do not proceed to the next proposition until the founder selects a final
disposition. Record confirmed meaning separately from the raw private answer.

## Completion standard

A Constitution-definition session is complete only when:

- every proposed item has a kind and decision owner;
- every confirmed rule has completed or explicitly parked all nine passes;
- every rephrase has a recorded final disposition;
- unresolved conflicts and affected-principal boundaries remain visible;
- no L3 candidate is described as enacted merely because the founder confirmed
  its meaning;
- the private proposal packet and public-safe operating interface remain
  separate.
