# Claim promotion checklist

Review each atomic claim separately.

1. **Provenance:** inspectable source or artifact and stable locator.
2. **Privacy/rights:** no secret or private payload; intended use is permitted.
3. **Atomicity:** one observation, decision, hypothesis, outcome, or system change.
4. **Graph fit:** mission, thesis, hypothesis, decision, experiment, outcome/eval, or system change.
5. **Verification:** command, grader, or observation is repeatable.
6. **Contradiction:** conflicting evidence and limitations are visible.
7. **Duplication:** merge or supersede rather than create parallel truth.
8. **Freshness:** time boundary and validity are explicit.
9. **Decision value:** the claim changes future retrieval or action.

Allowed dispositions: `promote`, `merge-existing`, `candidate`, `hold-incomplete`, `private-plane-only`, `reject`, `supersede`.
