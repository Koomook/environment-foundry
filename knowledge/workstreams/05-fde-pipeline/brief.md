# EF-05 — FDE pipeline

- Owner: talent lead
- Priority: P1
- Mission link: `knowledge/wiki/missions/company-environment-engine.md`
- Graph position: organizational hypothesis → experiment
- Objective: test whether a work sample selects people who reconstruct workflows accurately in the field.
- Inputs: task rubric, synthetic work sample, calibrated gold, later field outcome.
- Outputs: candidate rubric, blind scores, calibration and predictive-validity report.
- Owned paths: this workstream and `functions/06-talent/` artifacts.
- Canonical boundary: event participation is not a talent claim; only predictive evidence may be submitted.
- Privacy/rights boundary: candidate identity and raw performance remain private-plane-only.
- Baseline: self-report, résumé, and event participation alone.
- Metric and threshold: predeclared work-sample score predicts field quality and hard-failure rate on a held-out task.
- Stop rule: if the rubric does not predict field performance, stop using it for selection.
- External actions requiring founder approval: recruiting, offers, compensation, event-based selection.
