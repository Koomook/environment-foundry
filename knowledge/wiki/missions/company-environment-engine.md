# Company Environment Engine Mission

Environment Foundry builds an engine that repeatedly compiles authorized company operating events into rights-valid bounded task environments where AI can observe, act, and be evaluated.

Success is not the amount of data stored. It is valid environment production that improves outcome-adjusted performance, predicts prospective ranking, transfers beyond the originating operator or task, and makes the next environment cheaper and faster to build.

The current causal path is [[rights-aware-environment-foundry]]. The mission explicitly preserves [[environment-not-data-asset]] and [[rights-are-environment-semantics]].

## Boundary

This is not a claim that a complete company simulator or autonomous company exists. [[episode-replay-simulator-boundary]] defines the evidence required before stronger language is allowed.
