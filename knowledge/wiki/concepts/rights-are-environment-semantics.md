# Rights Are Environment Semantics

Rights determine which observations the agent may see, which actions it may execute, which outcomes may be retained, and whether evaluation, training, derivatives, resale, export, or deletion are allowed. They therefore change the task itself.

Ambiguity fails closed. Company ownership is not a substitute for scoped rights. Links: [[contract-access-precedes-acquisition]], [[company-environment-engine]].
