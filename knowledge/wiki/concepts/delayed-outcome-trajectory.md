# Delayed-Outcome Trajectory

A trajectory links what was observable at decision time, the decision and action taken, relationship context that was legally usable, later state changes, delayed outcomes, uncertainty, and provenance.

It is valuable raw material but not causal proof: only one historical policy path is observed and confounding remains. [[temporal-trajectories-create-training-value]] tests incremental value; [[episode-replay-simulator-boundary]] limits stronger claims.
