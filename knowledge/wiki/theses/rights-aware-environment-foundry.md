# Rights-Aware Environment Foundry Thesis

[[company-environment-engine]] can be advanced by combining field reconstruction, explicit rights, task and transition interfaces, leakage-resistant graders, prospective outcome checks, and reusable compiler contracts.

The first economic claim is not that raw company data is scarce. It is that the compiler can make a second valid environment faster and cheaper while retaining or improving predictive evaluation quality.

The thesis is tested through [[temporal-trajectories-create-training-value]], [[frontier-labs-buy-company-environments]], [[korea-operating-context-adds-lift]], and [[contract-access-precedes-acquisition]].

## Contradictions

The product may collapse into workflow software, data services, eval consulting, or buyer-internal tooling. RL is not assumed to be the first or necessary learning method. Generic model + private context + retrieval + harness remains a mandatory baseline.
