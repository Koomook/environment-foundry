# Frontier Labs Buy Company Environments

## Claim

A frontier-lab post-training, eval, data, or agent team will pay for a rights-valid company environment that meets its acceptance test.

## Test

Collect written task, tool, grader, provenance, rights, acceptance, budget-owner, and purchase-form specifications from named buyers.

## Threshold

Three buyer-written specifications or one paid design pilot covering fully loaded delivery cost.

## Stop rule

If the discovery window produces neither, hold the supplier thesis and focus on internal utility until buyer evidence changes.
