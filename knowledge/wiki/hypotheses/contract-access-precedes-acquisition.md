# Contract Access Precedes Acquisition

## Claim

Partnership and scoped licenses should precede ownership because company ownership does not automatically grant every observation, training, derivative, resale, export, retention, or third-party right.

## Test

Compare build, partner, license, minority-option, and ownership paths on rights-valid episode yield, required control, environment lift, full cost, and revocation risk.

## Threshold

Open acquisition only when contracts repeatedly fail to provide one specific necessary control, that control has demonstrated lift, the target works with data value set to zero, and ownership beats a matched contract arm.

## Stop rule

If contracts provide equivalent control or ownership economics fail, close acquisition and fund options.
