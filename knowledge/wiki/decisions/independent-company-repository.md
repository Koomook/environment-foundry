# Independent Company Repository Boundary

Environment Foundry is initialized as an independent repository rather than a Deep Thought subdirectory. This prevents Team Attention's mission, customer data, people, and historical claims from becoming this company's truth by filesystem proximity.

Selected predecessor research enters through a hashed source receipt and claim-level review. The operating architecture reuses the wiki → OKF → workstream pattern without copying the old company's canonical pages.
