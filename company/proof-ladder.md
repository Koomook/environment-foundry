# Proof ladder

Later claims remain closed until earlier gates pass.

| Gate | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| 0. Validity | Rights, provenance, schema, and leakage checks pass |
| 1. Reproduction | Two operators reproduce the same task contract |
| 2. Grader | Independent grader is reliable and catches hard failures |
| 3. Utility | Beats human-only, generic-model, retrieval, and rules baselines where relevant |
| 4. Prospective validity | Frozen temporal/entity holdout and shadow ranking work |
| 5. Live safety | Human-approved low-risk intervention; delayed outcomes close |
| 6. Simulator validity | Offline score predicts live policy ranking; exploitation tests pass |
| 7. Transfer | Works for a held-out operator, task, or company |
| 8. Foundry economics | Second adapter is measurably faster/cheaper with reusable contracts |
| 9. Demand | Three buyer-written specifications or one cost-covering paid design pilot |
| 10. Expansion | Only then test training lift, Korean locality, recompilation revenue, acquisition, or company-specific AI |
