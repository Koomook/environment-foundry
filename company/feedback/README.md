# Company Feedback System

The goal is to learn how real people understand, reject, or might participate in Environment Foundry without treating conversations as automatic research data.

## Two capture modes

### Fast capture — after a conversation

Record only:

- date and context;
- audience role or segment;
- company one-liner version heard;
- what became clear;
- strongest confusion or objection;
- concrete problem or desired outcome;
- next action and due date;
- permission: private notes / attributable internally / publishable;
- evidence class: opinion / observed behavior / stated commitment / completed action.

### Structured interview

Use the full template in `templates/conversation-feedback.md`. Do this only when the person has knowingly agreed to a research conversation.

## Feedback is not proof

| Input | What it can update | What it cannot prove |
|---|---|---|
| “Interesting” | Messaging resonance | Demand |
| Objection | Risk register and explanation | That the objection is universal |
| Request for follow-up | Relationship action | Purchase intent |
| Signed pilot or data-rights agreement | Commercial/rights evidence | Model or environment lift |
| Measured use and outcome | Product evidence | Cross-company transfer |

## Weekly synthesis

Every Friday, cluster feedback into:

1. repeated confusion;
2. repeated urgent problem;
3. rights or trust blocker;
4. willingness to provide access, time, or money;
5. claim that should be deleted;
6. experiment that should run next.

Promote only atomic, source-backed, privacy-safe claims through the evidence compiler. Keep names and raw conversation text outside the public/canonical plane.
