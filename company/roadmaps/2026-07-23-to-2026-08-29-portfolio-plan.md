## The research working group launched on Jul 23

Working name: **Ralphthon Auto Research Working Group**

Initial questions:

1. Can a deterministic operational grader predict independent human judgment on held-out release decisions?
2. Which representation of state → decision → action → delayed outcome remains useful across operators or organizations?
3. What minimum rights and provenance package makes an episode usable, revocable, reproducible, and publishable?

Required roles before external launch: research lead, environment owner, rights/privacy owner, independent grader lead, and paper editor. Authorship follows documented intellectual and experimental contribution; participation or data provision alone does not silently transfer IP or imply authorship.
