# Non-goals

The following are not current products or claims:

- operating a fully autonomous company;
- a perfect company digital twin or founder clone;
- guaranteeing full-company AX outcomes;
- treating recordings, messages, faces, or contacts as ready-made RL data;
- using one opaque scalar reward for company performance;
- treating plausible role-play as causal counterfactual truth;
- selling company-specific foundation-model weights today;
- starting with a fund, PE vehicle, or company acquisition;
- opening community, events, media, and the FDE network as separate businesses at once.

Acquisition remains an option only after contract-based access repeatedly fails to obtain a control right that has demonstrated environment value and ownership wins a matched economic test.
